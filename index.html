National Yuanshan Archaeological Site

Learn More about the Archaeological Work

As the Japanese rule came to an end, archaeological work initiated by the Japanese archaeologists in Yuanshan was carried on by the Department of Anthropology of National Taiwan University. Guess what the archaeological team found at that time?

They found that the site was occupied by different groups of people at various time periods since early prehistory. Evidence of their daily lives was accumulated and deposited to form multiple cultural layers. There are seven layers from bottom to top: a Pre-ceramic layer, followed by the Tapenkeng , Hsuntangpu , Yuanshan , Chihwuyuan (Botanical Garden), and Shihsanhang layers, and the Han layer. The earliest Pre-ceramic culture indicates human activities in this area as early as ten thousand years ago.
